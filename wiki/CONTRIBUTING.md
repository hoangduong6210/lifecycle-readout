# Contributing

Read `AGENTS.md` and [Start Here](START-HERE.md) before changes. Keep reusable
implementation under `src/`, thin study entry points under `experiments/`, and
authoritative parameters under `configs/` or `protocols/`. Do not import from a
sibling project. Update [Project Status](status/Project-Status.md) when blockers
or scientific state change. Preserve both positive and negative interventions.

The [hard rule in AGENTS.md, section 9](../AGENTS.md#9-wiki-and-paper-snapshots)
requires wiki-first research updates. Do not update or export a manuscript for
every finding. Assemble the paper from the reconciled wiki and verified evidence
at the final manuscript stage, or export a working draft on explicit user request.
