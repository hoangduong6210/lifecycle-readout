# Research Workflow

```text
question -> frozen readout/intervention protocol -> scheduler execution
-> complete attempts -> ancestry/invariance/intervention audit
-> immutable release -> evidence entry -> scoped claim -> paper snapshot
```

Heavy training is scheduler-only. Negative or failed interventions remain
visible. No mutable artifact is copied directly into a paper snapshot.
