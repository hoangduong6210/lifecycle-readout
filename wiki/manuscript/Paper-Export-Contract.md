# Paper Export Contract

`paper/working/` is mutable and non-canonical. A submission snapshot requires an admitted claim
set, current frozen evidence release, source wiki commit, figure and bibliography
hashes, toolchain identity, anonymous-variant leak scan, and
`results.lock.yaml`. The resulting `paper/snapshots/<snapshot-id>/` is immutable.

Routine research updates stay in the wiki under the
[wiki-first hard rule](../../AGENTS.md#9-wiki-and-paper-snapshots). Assemble a
final manuscript from reconciled wiki content and verified evidence at the final
stage. An earlier working export requires an explicit user request; it remains
exploratory and must not be labeled a frozen submission snapshot. Keep existing
Word versions as historical exports rather than a competing current narrative.
