# Paper Export Contract

`paper/working/` is mutable and non-canonical. Export requires an admitted claim
set, current frozen evidence release, source wiki commit, figure and bibliography
hashes, toolchain identity, anonymous-variant leak scan, and
`results.lock.yaml`. The resulting `paper/snapshots/<snapshot-id>/` is immutable.
