# Project Limitations

- No frozen scientific results release or admitted claim exists.
- Legacy benchmark outputs and later corrected candidate-stream studies remain
  exploratory. Earlier namespace/history defects and later information-access
  confounds have distinct scopes; apparent gains do not establish lifecycle
  efficacy or generalization.
- Dormant phase, latent phase count, silence direction, and ordered survival
  affinities are imposed design choices. Monotonicity is an architectural prior,
  not causal discovery.
- The pure-silence theorem concerns a fixed affinity, fixed initial encoder state
  and exact decay flow. The candidate transformation is evaluated before the
  label is observed; it is not a recorded renewal at link arrival. Even a constant
  transformation can reverse the complete scored response. The restricted
  condition and constructive counterexample are in the gating method.
- Internal phase replacements and silence perturbations are model interventions;
  observational event logs do not by themselves identify real-world causal effects.
- The dormant anchor and ordering of gate weights are imposed. Neither boundary
  nor intermediate components have established external semantics or cross-seed
  semantic stability. Encoder-state summaries do not establish scored-state meaning.
- Exponential attenuation, probability floors and finite-precision saturation can
  suppress sensitivity. The clamp may act before actual underflow. Their
  contribution to predictive losses has not been isolated; a different clock
  remains a separately controlled hypothesis.
- Phase profiles are descriptive, not external semantic labels. Cross-domain
  meaning and cross-seed phase invariance remain open questions.
- Official baselines require shared split, candidate negatives, feature access,
  validation selection, and compute accounting before comparative claims.
- Dataset license/source review and independently reproducible corpus identity
  remain incomplete. Legacy CoEdit redistribution remains separately blocked.
- A passing structural test suite or matching checksum does not admit scientific
  conclusions. Smoke runs are pipeline checks and must not enter paper tables.
- Manuscript working drafts and numerical literature rows remain quarantined
  pending citation, results, and publication-gate review.

- Earlier recurrence/silence strata were defined on positive events only; paired
  negatives need not have matching history. They do not establish that binary
  recurrence discrimination has been eliminated.
- Hard controls condition on matched-bin recurrent support, exclude first-time pairs,
  and train on a much smaller subset. They cannot estimate full-stream utility
  or first-time-link recovery. The fixed scaled clock is a separate amendment.
- A connected task graph permits but does not guarantee nonzero gradients.
  Unconstrained gates can satisfy monotonicity; inversion is not inevitable.
- Neutral passthrough returns the current jointly trained backbone's score,
  not necessarily the score or AP of an independently trained base predictor.
- Decay underflow and clamp saturation are hypotheses about performance until
  isolated in controlled experiments; a small learned transition norm alone
  does not identify their cause.

- Shared feature access and optimization budgets do not equalize model capacity
  or lifecycle regularization. The hard-control comparison does not isolate the
  temporal constraint independently from these implementation choices.
- Supplied ML-20M masks are retained. Upstream time cutoffs precede node
  filtering; source inspection has not reconstructed original MovieLens bytes
  into the supplied benchmark file.
