# Research Questions

- `LCR-RQ-DECODER-001`: Does hierarchical factorization make DECAY reachable
  without collapsing the lifecycle distribution?
- `LCR-RQ-INVARIANCE-001`: Is the interpretable branch a non-ancestor of the
  scored output on the admitted implementation?
- `LCR-RQ-INTERVENTION-001`: Do typed state and driver interventions satisfy
  declared direction and reversibility gates?
- `LCR-RQ-DISCOVERY-001`: Can continuous latent lifecycle states and transition operators be discovered directly from continuous-time event sequences without designer-imposed state priors?
- `LCR-RQ-SHORTCUT-001`: Does incorporating the discovered latent lifecycle as a survival filter mitigate frequency shortcut artifacts and enhance inductive link prediction?
- `LCR-RQ-CAUSAL-001`: Does continuous time-silence counterfactual intervention induce a monotonic decay response without hand-initialized weights?

All remain pending formal evidence-release review after migration.

## Operationalized corrective question

For the existing `LCR-RQ-SHORTCUT-001`, the prospective
`LCR-P-HARD-001` comparison asks whether ordered phase dynamics improve pooled
AP over Base plus identical pair statistics, scalar decay and MLP gating on
common historically observed candidates matched by recurrence and silence.
This narrower estimand distinguishes feature access from gate architecture;
it does not resolve unrestricted lifecycle discovery or real-world causality.
Results and support denominators belong in the results wiki, and only reviewed
frozen evidence can change the admitted claim language.


## Prediction target before the next research study

`LCR-RQ-TARGET-001`: Which improvements concern official candidate ranking,
sampled-label discrimination, population probability or time-to-event likelihood?
The [target and sampling boundary](../methods/Prediction-Target-and-Sampling.md)
separates these estimands. Existing gates do not identify relation survival from
sampled edge classification alone. Any new objective requires a prospective
amendment before fitting or inspecting new test outcomes.
