Supplementary aggregate data

Article: Monotonicity of Temporal Graph Readouts: Exact Certificates and Predictive Limits

These files contain aggregate mathematical examples and model-evaluation summaries. They contain no user-level interaction records, model checkpoints or source datasets. AP and AUC use the unit interval unless explicitly labelled as percentage points (pp). Across-seed SD uses the sample definition. Seeds index trained model/candidate realizations, not independent populations.

Figure3_data.csv: safe/unsafe readout curves and the separate redistribution-coordinate example. The final column is the readout for the first two configurations and coordinate q1 for the redistribution configuration.
Figure4_data.csv: raw and projected coordinates for the exact synthetic halfspace example. The specified-rate boundary is x1 + 2*x2 = 1; the all-rate lower bound is x2 >= sqrt(1/2).
Figure6_data.csv: threshold-crossing counts and candidate denominators for every dataset, seed and displayed path.
Figure7_data.csv: changed-tilt counts and same-coordinate squared distances.
Figure8_data.csv: January exploratory and February fixed-follow-up seed differences, in AP units.
Figure8_interval.csv: seed means, conditional February interval, query counts and active-user counts. January has no reported confidence interval.
Table1_coverage.csv: event and query support counts.
Table2_paths.csv: the complete displayed path comparison.
Table3_repairs.csv: AP for original dynamic, raw gradient and both same-metric repairs, plus their deformation.
Table4_prediction.csv: all seed-level pooled test AP/AUC results, selection epochs and parameter counts.
LastFM_all_cohorts.csv: all 16 fixed cohorts, six arms, three seeds and both months, including AP, AUC and pair accuracy.
LastFM_all_contrasts.csv: all five paired AP contrasts in every fixed cohort, seed and month. Cohort names encode the manuscript's gap/dose bins; 'dose_1_to_9' means [1,10), and 'all' means the complete supported population. Cohorts overlap and must not be summed.

January was used for model selection and exploratory subgroup discovery. February used the fixed selected models and prespecified follow-up cohort; its local confirmation screen was not met. The February percentile interval conditions on the frozen models and observed active-user population and does not include optimizer uncertainty or cross-user graph dependence.

Figures 1, 2 and 5 are pipeline/certificate schematics; Figure 4 is analytic geometry; their labels and mathematical definitions are given in the article. Original interaction datasets are governed by their providers' terms and are not redistributed in this package.
